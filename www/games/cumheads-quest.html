THE CRYPT OF THE RED MOON

Walk into monsters to fight. Keyboard: arrows or WASD to move, Space or Z is A (wait / stairs), B or Enter opens the pack. In menus use the arrows, Enter to choose and Esc to go back. A gamepad works too, and so does touch. Monsters move when you do, and you get hungry. 50 floors, a boss every 6, and a 1 in 10 chance of a rest stop between floors (pizza, chilli, inns, a tavern, weapons, and a chill octopus DJ). Level up to pick your stats.

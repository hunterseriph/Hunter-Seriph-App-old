Cumheads Quest

THE CRYPT OF THE RED MOON

How to play

Controls

Arrows or WASD move. Space or Z waits and takes stairs. B or Enter opens the pack, Esc goes back. Gamepad and touch work too. Walk into foes to fight them.

Clubhouse Row

Walk into a door to go inside. Switch character at the Clubhouse, spend Cumhead tokens in the Spermaid Sanctum, then take the stairs down.

The Crypt

50 floors and a boss every 6. Rest stops on the way down: chat, buy, or stop for a Combat Stim to earn reputation. At 100% you can hire that character.

Tokens and your egg

Tokens are yours forever. Your egg gains XP as you do and hatches at level 5.
